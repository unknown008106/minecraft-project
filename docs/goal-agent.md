# Goal agent

Plan only, nothing here is built yet. Taken from the main project's crafting plan.

His picture, from his dictated message. He asks an agent for a diamond pickaxe. It first checks its own inventory, then asks the owner in chat for what would shorten the job (a pickaxe, food), says it is waiting 10 seconds, and goes on alone if the owner has nothing or stays quiet. Alone, it punches wood, makes a crafting table and a wooden pickaxe, mines cobblestone for a stone pickaxe, finds and smelts iron for an iron pickaxe, finds diamonds by strip mining and branch mining, and makes the diamond pickaxe. Beyond the order of steps, it plans where it goes, never too far, staying safe. Before a long trip it plans food, asks the owner for food first and gathers its own if needed. A short job like a crafting table needs no food. During the job it switches profiles whenever it needs to. A skeleton shows up while it holds an axe or a pickaxe and no shield, so it switches to the fighter without a shield. With a shield and an axe it fights the axe way. Once the skeleton is dead it switches straight back to the strip miner. About 4 profiles together complete 1 overarching task.

How much of it exists. The crew's mode manager (bridge/crew/behaviors.js, modes.js) already switches profiles by priority every tick, with kill, retreat, guard, eat, give, come, build, mine, explore, farm, follow and idle, so fighting a mob in the middle of mining and going back to mining is already its shape. Its fight profile runs the trained network through skills/fight.js. skills/food.js already gathers food before a long job, but a flat 10 food points for any job. The new miner (skills/mine_branch.js) does branch mining with lava handling.

What is missing, in build order. First, the goal planner on top of the crafting planner, which turns "diamond pickaxe" into ordered steps and hands each one to the right profile (gather, craft, mine, fight, survive). Second, the ask the owner step, where it lists the items that cut the most steps, asks once in chat, announces the 10 second wait, takes anything tossed to it and goes on at the timeout. Third, the trip budget. It estimates the job's length from the plan (blocks to dig for diamonds at their 1.21 depth, walking, smelting time) and turns that into a food need from Minecraft's hunger cost of mining, sprinting, jumping and fighting, so a crafting table needs no food and a diamond trip does. It also gets a range limit from home and a return rule (low health, food running out, inventory full, night if unsafe). Fourth, a fighter picked by kit. The selector from docs/fighting-profiles-selector.md reads the hands and inventory, so a shield and an axe get the axe fighter and no shield gets a fighter built for no shield. The fight ends with the kill, and the saved step resumes (the tunnel position and direction for the strip miner).

Two checks before it ships. Our fighters trained against players and have never been tested against skeletons or other mobs since the zombie benchmarks were dropped, so a mob fight check comes before the fight profile takes mobs, with the crew's simpler mob fighting as the fallback. This switching is between profiles inside a long job, and his earlier call that the fighting selector picks once per duel, with no switching mid fight, still stands.
